How the rankings are built

Each model buys the priced portion of the latest disclosed long equity book at the close of the next trading day after filing. Positions drift with prices until the next disclosure. A model stops when a disclosure is over 155 days old or less than 75% of the disclosed equity value has usable prices. Rankings require coverage on every trading day in the comparison window.

Returns use dividend- and split-adjusted constituent prices in USD. The composite averages directional percentiles across ten return, risk and concentration measures. Sharpe and alpha use a 4.5% annual risk-free rate. Trailing P/E is a dated holdings-level valuation and shows the share of the book with eligible multiples.

13F reconstruction covers reportable long equity. Cash, shorts, derivatives, private positions, funds’ actual trades and fees can change the performance investors experienced. The holdings archive preserves the disclosed instrument types. SEC Form 13F documentation.
