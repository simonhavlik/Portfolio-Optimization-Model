Roadmap

The board is curated today. Automation removes the curation, not the comparison.

Shipped

  • Risk engine: frontier, drawdown, CVaR, factors, Monte Carlo
  • 13F books reconstructed quarter by quarter, weights fixed at filing
  • Ten funds ranked on ten measures, with per-fund pages and CSV export

Next — mass ingestion

  • Walk every active 13F filer rather than a chosen list
  • Incremental quarterly pulls, amendments deduplicated by period
  • Validation gates: coverage, orphan CUSIPs, implausible value jumps
  • Throttled to the SEC limit with backoff, so a crawl cannot be banned

Later — cohorts & cadence

  • Cohorts by AUM and style: a $400m manager is not ranked against Berkshire
  • Persistence tests: does the ranking survive a rolling window
  • Scheduled overnight rebuild, exports regenerated, changes diffed

Constraints

  • 45-day lag13Fs arrive six weeks after quarter end; the board is a record, not a signal.
  • Long equity onlyNo shorts, options, cash or non-US lines are disclosed, so a book is a floor on exposure.
  • Values, not sharesWeights come from disclosed values; splits are handled through the price series.
  • No back-fillEvery metric uses filings public at the time, or it would be look-ahead.
