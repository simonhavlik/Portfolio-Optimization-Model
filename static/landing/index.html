Why I built this

I wanted the analysis I do for my own book to take a minute, not a week.

So I built it. One upload decomposes exposure, drawdown and tail risk across every position, stresses the strategy against volatility regimes rather than a flattering backtest, and pushes the book toward a frontier I can defend — for me, and for the friends who trust me with theirs.
